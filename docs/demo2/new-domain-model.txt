Invitation validation by type:
- `INITIAL_ORGANISATION_ADMIN`, `ORGANISATION_TRAINEE`, and `ORGANISATION_ADMIN_PROMOTION` require `organisationId`.
- `ORGANISATION_ADMIN_PROMOTION` requires `targetUserId` to refer to an active organisation trainee in the same organisation.
- `PLATFORM_ADMIN` and `PLATFORM_ADMIN_UPGRADE` must not be scoped to an organisation.
